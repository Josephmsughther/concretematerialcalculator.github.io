Assumptions & Defaults
  • Dry Volume Factor: 1.54
  • Cement Density: 1,440 kg/m³
  • Cement Bag Weight: 50 kg
  • Sand Bulk Density: 1,600 kg/m³
  • Aggregate Bulk Density: 1,450 kg/m³
  • Mix ratios follow BS/Eurocode nominal mix conventions (C10–C30)

These are standard reference values. Actual material densities can vary with moisture content and source — verify against site-specific data for critical structural work.

How to Use
  1. Choose your Calculation Method — Direct Volume if you already know the volume, or By Dimensions if you only have length, width, and depth.
  2. Fill in the fields relevant to your chosen method.
  3. Select your Concrete Grade — the mix ratio is applied automatically.
  4. Adjust Wastage % if your site requires a different allowance.
  5. Toggle the unit for Sand and Aggregate between m³, kg, and tons — results update instantly.
